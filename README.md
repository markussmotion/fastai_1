# fastai_1
My Journey through Practical Deep Learning - part 1

# Lesson 1
I have a huge garden and I am sometimes actually concerned as to if in particular the bigger trees are actually healthy. I heard that fungus growth somewhat indicates health depending on which kind of fungus is present. Also marks and injuries of the bark might give some insight. For the first lesson I therefore decided to classify trees by looking at the bark. The resnet18 model has decent accuracy being trained in 8 epochs on just a handful of pictures. Data was not checked beforehand, so some images might be unfit.

# Lesson 2
For an identifier on hf spaces I decided to grab image datasets of digimon and pokemon to train a resnet model on those. The result works as intended even though the actual results are not satisfactory.
